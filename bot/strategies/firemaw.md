# Firemaw (Blackwing Lair, boss 4)
- Flame Buffet stacks on everyone in line of sight: ranged/healers hide behind pillars/corner to drop stacks, then come back.
- Tank in a spot where raid can LOS him. Wing Buffet knocks back and lowers threat — 2 tanks.
- Shadow Flame frontal: never stand in front. Onyxia Scale Cloak required.
