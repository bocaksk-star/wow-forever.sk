# Shazzrah (Molten Core, boss 5)
- Blinks to a random player and wipes threat: whole raid stays close so tanks can re-pick him fast.
- Arcane Explosion around him: casters spread a bit, melee accept damage.
- Shazzrah's Curse (doubles magic damage taken): decurse.
- Deaden Magic on himself: priests/shamans dispel/purge it.
- Counterspell: casters, don't spam long casts right after a blink.
