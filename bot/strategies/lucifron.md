# Lucifron (Molten Core, boss 1)
- 2 Flamewaker Protector adds: tank them separately, kill adds first.
- Lucifron's Curse (doubles mana costs) and Impending Doom: mages/druids decurse immediately.
- Dominate Mind: mind-controlled player — sheep/polymorph or CC them, do not kill.
- Shadow Shock is frontal-ish, keep melee behind.
