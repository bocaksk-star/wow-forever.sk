# Onyxia (Onyxia's Lair) — 3 phases
## Phase 1 (100–65%)
- Tank turns her to face the back wall; nobody stands in front (Flame Breath) or behind (Tail Sweep).
- Melee on her flanks only. Ranged spread along the side walls.
- Tank threat matters: Wing Buffet reduces threat, so DPS hold back first 10 sec.
## Phase 2 (65–40%) — she flies
- Ranged + healers DPS her from the sides. Melee kill whelps that spawn from side caves.
- DEEP BREATH: when she inhales, move to the sides of the room, never the middle line she faces.
- Fireballs hit random players, stay spread 8+ yards.
## Phase 3 (40–0%) — she lands
- Tank picks her up again, same positioning as P1.
- Bellowing Roar fears everyone: Fear Ward / Tremor Totem / Fear immunity on tank; do NOT get feared into the floor eruptions near lava cracks.
- Whelps keep coming briefly, AoE them.
## Gear/prep
- Onyxia Scale Cloak not needed (that's for Nefarian). Fire resistance helps healers vs Fireballs.
