# Vaelastrasz the Corrupt (Blackwing Lair, boss 2)
- Short, intense DPS race. Everyone gets Essence of the Red (infinite mana/energy/rage) — burn everything.
- BURNING ADRENALINE: target dies after ~20s — if on a tank, run to a safe spot AWAY from the raid before you die (it explodes).
- Tanks: queue of 3+ tanks, swap as one gets Adrenaline. Off-tanks stay 2nd/3rd on threat.
- Raid stands to his side; Flame Breath frontal and Tail Swipe behind.
- Fire resistance helps for Fire Nova.
