# Baron Geddon (Molten Core, boss 6)
- LIVING BOMB: if you get it, RUN AWAY from the raid immediately (to a marked spot) and wait for it to explode.
- Inferno: melee run out when he channels.
- Ignite Mana burns mana: dispel it from healers.
- At low health he does Armageddon-style big damage; keep raid topped up.
