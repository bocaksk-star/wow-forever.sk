# Razorgore the Untamed (Blackwing Lair, boss 1)
- Phase 1: one player MIND CONTROLS Razorgore (via Orb) and destroys 30 dragon eggs.
- Rest of raid holds adds in corners: assign tanks to each side (left/right), kill dragonkin & legionnaires, AoE whelps.
- Mages/priests on ranged mobs, warriors on mage adds that cast fireballs on the controller.
- Phase 2: after all eggs destroyed, Razorgore becomes hostile; tank him in a corner, Conflagration on tank — swap tanks if needed.
