# Chromaggus (Blackwing Lair, boss 7)
- Two random breaths each week: Time Lapse, Corrosive Acid, Ignite Flesh, Incinerate, Frost Burn. Hide behind the gate/pillars when he breathes.
- Brood Affliction debuffs (5 colors): dispel/cleanse fast. Bronze = stun: needs Hourglass Sand item. 5 colors at once = Chromatic Mutation (MC) — don't let it happen.
- Frenzy: Tranquilizing Shot. Shimmer changes vulnerability: casters check which school does damage.
- At 20% he enrages, burn hard.
