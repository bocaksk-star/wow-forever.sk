# Majordomo Executus (Molten Core, boss 9)
- 8 adds: 4 Flamewaker Healers + 4 Flamewaker Elites. Kill adds; Majordomo surrenders when they die.
- Assign each add a tank or CC: mages polymorph, warlocks banish not possible — focus kill order: Healers first.
- Magic Reflection & Damage Shield: when shields are up, casters/melee STOP hitting the shielded mobs.
- Teleports a player into the fire pit area: that player runs back.
- After he surrenders, talk to him to summon Ragnaros.
