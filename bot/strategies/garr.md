# Garr (Molten Core, boss 4)
- 8 Firesworn adds. Off-tanks / warlocks banish (warlocks banish 1-2 each), rest tanked by off-tanks.
- Kill Garr first while adds are held/banished; do NOT kill adds near the raid — they explode (Eruption) and increase Garr's damage.
- If killing adds, do it one at a time away from the raid.
- Antimagic Pulse dispels buffs — rebuff tanks.
