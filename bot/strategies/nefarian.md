# Nefarian (Blackwing Lair, final boss)
- Phase 1: Lord Victor Nefarius — kill drakonid adds from both sides (colors vary). 42 adds total, tanks on each side, AoE. Mind control and Shadowbolt Volley from Nefarius.
- Phase 2: Nefarian lands. Tank him facing away; Shadow Flame frontal (Onyxia Scale Cloak!), Bellowing Roar fear (Fear Ward/tremor totem).
- CLASS CALLS: every ~30s he calls one class: Warrior (Berserker stance), Priest (heals damage), Mage (polymorph random), Rogue (teleported in front), Druid (cat form), Shaman (totems spawn), Warlock (infernals), Hunter (bows break), Paladin (Blessing of Protection on him). React to your class call.
- Phase 3 (20%): skeletons of dead adds rise — AoE them and finish Nefarian.
