# Magmadar (Molten Core, boss 2)
- Panic (AoE fear every ~30s): Fear Ward on main tank, Tremor Totems, Berserker Rage for warriors.
- Frenzy: hunters must Tranquilizing Shot immediately — assign 2 hunters in rotation.
- Lava Bomb: move out of fire patches on the ground.
- Tank faces him away from the raid (Conflagration in front).
