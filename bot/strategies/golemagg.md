# Golemagg the Incinerator (Molten Core, boss 7)
- 2 Core Rager dogs: off-tanks hold them away from Golemagg; dogs can't die while Golemagg lives — ignore them.
- Golemagg gains stacking damage on tank (Magma Splash): use 2 tanks swapping if stacks get high.
- Pyroblast on random players: healers watch.
- Earthquake at 10%: melee step out.
