# Ragnaros (Molten Core, final boss)
- Fire resistance gear: tanks aim for ~250+, healers/casters as much as possible.
- Wrath of Ragnaros: knockback around main tank — have a 2nd tank ready to pick him up; melee stand so knockback doesn't throw you into lava.
- Melee: back off before Wrath (~every 25-30s), then return.
- Lava Burst: ranged spread, stay away from eruptions.
- At 3 min (or when he submerges): 8 Sons of Flame spawn. Kill them fast — mages/warlocks AoE, everyone help. He re-emerges after 90 sec or when Sons die.
- Goal: kill him before the first submerge if DPS is good enough.
