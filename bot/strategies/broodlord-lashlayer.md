# Broodlord Lashlayer (Blackwing Lair, boss 3)
- Suppression room before him: rogues disarm traps or players run through carefully; kill adds as they come.
- Broodlord: Mortal Strike on tank — strong heals needed. Knock Away reduces threat: 2 tanks swapping.
- Blast Wave: melee spread a little; tank him against a wall.
